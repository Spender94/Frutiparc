In short

Frutiparc is a volunteer-run, non-commercial games site: a re-creation of a 2000s fruit park. We only collect what's needed to play. No ads, no trackers, no selling of data — ever. You can download everything the park knows about you, and delete your account yourself, from the app's settings.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is {{RESPONSABLE}}. For any question, request or complaint about your data, write to: {{CONTACT}}. We reply within a month.

The site has no data protection officer: it isn't required to have one (it is not a public authority, does no large-scale monitoring and handles no sensitive data), and the address above is enough to answer you.

Signing up asks for only three things: a username, a secret code, and — if you like — an e-mail. No name, no age, no phone number. Everything else is up to you to fill in.

The secret code is stored hashed (bcrypt): nobody, not even the team, can read it.

What others see of you

Your username, your frutibouille, your level, your scores and your medals are public: that's the game. Your profile (first name, last name, city, occupation, website, comment) is public too — everything in it is optional, and you can change it whenever you like. If you entered your date of birth, others only see your age — never the date — and nothing at all under 18. Your e-mail, your IP address and your secret code are never shown.

Age

Frutiparc re-creates a 2000s park for those who knew it: it is aimed at an adult audience. So we don't ask for your date of birth — that would mean collecting one more piece of data from everyone, and we'd rather keep only what's strictly necessary. It remains optional, in your profile, if you want your age to be shown.

That said, French law requires a minor under 15 to sign up only with the consent of one of their parents (or of their legal guardian): if that's you, ask them before creating your account. A parent who wants to see, correct or delete their child's data can write to us at the address above — we reply within a month.

Your rights, and where to exercise them

  • Access and portability — in the app, Settings → My data → Download my data: a file with everything the server holds on your account, table by table.
  • Rectification — your profile can be edited at any time (Edit my profile); for your username or e-mail, write to us.
  • Erasure (deleting your account) — Settings → My data → Delete my account, with your secret code. The account is erased 7 days later; logging back in before then cancels the request. Your public messages (forum, chat rooms) remain readable, but under the name “compte_supprime”: they no longer identify you. Your username is reserved forever, so that nobody can pass themselves off as you.
  • Objection and restriction — write to us: we process nothing that isn't necessary for the game, but every request is looked into.
  • Withdrawal of consent — delete your e-mail or your profile, turn off notifications: the effect is immediate.
  • Complaint — if you believe your rights are not being respected, you can refer the matter to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr/fr/plaintes). We'd rather you wrote to us first, but it's your right.

Who it passes through

The data is not sold, rented, or passed on to third parties for their own use. Four technical services see it go by:

  • The host of the server and of its database, on servers located in the European Union.
  • Resend (e-mail delivery) — your address, only for a forgotten secret code and the inactivity warning. Resend is an American company: the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Your browser's notification service (Google, Apple or Mozilla depending on your device) — an encrypted delivery address that it cannot read itself, and only if you turned notifications on.
  • YouTube, for the chat-room blind tests — and only if you click “Listen”. Nothing is loaded before your click. The player comes from youtube-nocookie.com, which doesn't set advertising cookies; YouTube still receives your IP address for the length of the clip. “Mute” removes the player, and your choice is remembered on your device until you mute it.

On your device

The site keeps locally (localStorage) your login session, a few display preferences, your blind test choice, and a device token (a randomly drawn number that says nothing about you or your device) — that's what saves you from retyping your secret code, and what makes it possible to recognise two accounts opened from the same browser. These are strictly necessary data: no cookie banner is required. No third-party cookies, no audience measurement tools, no ad networks.

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed secret codes, access to the administration restricted to the team and logged. In the event of a data breach that would put you at risk, you would be informed, and so would the CNIL within 72 hours.
